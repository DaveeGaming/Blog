Making a roulette wheel in Minecraft

Date: 2025-06-13

Have you ever wanted to live out your gambling dreams in minecraft? Felt the need to make some bets with friends for the hell of it? Well, do I have the solution to your problems!

Living the two week Minecraft phase with friends always leads to some of the most random and amazing moments of my gaming carrier. This time, the idea of making a functional casino was brought up, and I took on the challenge instantly. Our small little server is not vanilla, we used Create: Ultimate Selection as a base, and added CC: Tweaked by my recommendation, It's genuinely one of my favourite mods to date. I decided to use Create with Computercraft and see if I could figure out a way to make a really fancy and working roulette wheel.

If you haven't heard of Create yet, I really urge you to look into a few videos about the mod, it brought a lot of life into minecraft and the possibilities it creates are endless. But don't worry, I will explain all the nessecary details about the mod!

How Create works

Create uses minecraft Entities to make awesome moving and interactive crafts called contraptions. As opposed to static, solid blocks, entities are a lot more dynamic, and a lot more complicated. Every mob, enemy, villagers, arrows, even items on the ground are all entities. These have a lot more restrictions as to how they can interact with the world, and these show in create contraptions.

source: https://create.fandom.com/wiki/Contraption

Create uses these contraptions to free itself from the minecraft grid, allowing to build any kind of structure from solid blocks, and move them at very specific angles.

Limits of contraptions

As the contraption's blocks are not solid, they hardly interact with the solid minecraft world. Create exposes a lot of special blocks, which still have functionality on contraptions, for example functional doors, seats, drills, etc. For our need, we only need a big disk spinning in a circle, and a mechanical bearing does exactly that.
